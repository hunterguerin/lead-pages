## What's Inside

This guide walks you through **every step** to turn your Shopify store into an online ordering system for firewood and bulk material delivery. You'll learn how to **add products with the right pricing structure** (per cord, per yard, per ton), **set up Local Delivery zones** with distance-based fees so customers outside your range never see the delivery option, and **enable prepayment** so every order is paid before your driver loads up.

The guide also covers the math on why prepayment pays for itself on day one - if you average just 2 no-shows per week at $250/delivery, that's **$26,000/year in wasted trips** that disappear the moment you require checkout payment. Plus, you'll get a list of **alternative platforms** (Zapiet, BulkDelivery PRO, Haulstr, Square Online) in case Shopify isn't the right fit for your operation.

## Who This Is For

This guide is built for **firewood sellers, landscape supply yards, and aggregate delivery businesses** who are tired of phone tag, no-shows, and chasing payments after the truck has already left. Whether you already have a Shopify store or you're starting fresh, this guide gets you from zero to live online ordering in a single afternoon. No developer needed - every step tells you exactly where to click.
