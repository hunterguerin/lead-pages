## What's Inside

A full cord is a **legally defined measurement — 128 cubic feet** of tightly stacked wood. Everything else is just a fraction of that number. This guide gives you the **exact cubic feet for every firewood size** (full, half, third, quarter, and sixth cord) plus the **honest rack dimensions** for each one.

Here's the part most buyers never learn: a "rack" or "face cord" only describes the *wall* of wood you see — not how deep it's stacked. With standard 16-inch wood, a **4x8 rack sold as a "half cord" is really only a third** — leaving you **33% short, every single time**. The guide shows you the math, the weasel words to watch for, and a **10-second way to measure any stack** and check it against your invoice.
