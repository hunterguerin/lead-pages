This guide walks you through **every click** needed to launch a Google Search ad campaign for a local firewood business. You'll get a **complete keyword list** with the right match types to catch real buyers (not researchers), **exact location targeting settings** so you only pay for clicks from people in your delivery area, and a **proven ad copy template** you can customize in 2 minutes.
